  \ipa{u} & short close back rounded vowel \\

  \ipa{f} & voiceless labiodental fricative \\
  \ipa{T} & voiceless dental fricative \\
  \ipa{D} & voiced dental fricative \\

  \ipa{s\super Q} & voiceless pharyngealized alveolar fricative (emphatic) \\
